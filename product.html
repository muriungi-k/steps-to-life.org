Product Description

The Great Controversy traces the conflict between Christ and Satan throughout history. It explores biblical prophecy, the Protestant Reformation, religious liberty, and God's plan for the final events of earth's history. This inspiring Christian classic encourages readers to prepare for Christ's soon return and to place their trust completely in God.

This book has inspired millions of readers around the world through its clear explanation of Scripture, historical events, and the everlasting gospel. It is an excellent resource for personal study, evangelism, and Bible study groups.
