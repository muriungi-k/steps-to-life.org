STEPS TO LIFE — SEVEN-DAY BIBLE READING PLAN

Day 1: Genesis 1 — God the Creator
Day 2: Psalm 23 — God our Shepherd
Day 3: Matthew 5 — The teachings of Jesus
Day 4: Luke 15 — God's love for the lost
Day 5: John 15 — Remaining in Christ
Day 6: Romans 8 — Life and hope in the Spirit
Day 7: Revelation 21 — The promise of a new creation

Each day:
1. Pray for understanding.
2. Read the chapter in your Bible.
3. Write down a verse that stands out to you.
4. Ask what the passage reveals about God.
5. Choose one practical response and close in prayer.
